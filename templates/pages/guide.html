Launch Guide

A short checklist to get the most out of your launch.

Before you submit

  1. Write a sharp tagline. One line (max 140 characters) that says what it does and for whom.
  2. Prepare visuals. A square logo (under 2 MB) and up to 3 screenshots (under 3 MB each) that show the product in use.
  3. Explain the “why”. In the description, cover the problem, who it helps and what makes it different.
  4. Check your link. Make sure your website works on mobile and a visitor can try the product quickly.

Choose when to launch

Launch as soon as you are approved, or schedule a date and time. Many makers pick a weekday morning when more people are online.

Review

We review every submission, usually within 48 hours. If something is missing we email you and show a note on your dashboard. Fix it and resubmit.

Go further

  • Premium launch: a gold badge and priority review. See Pricing.
  • Advertising: pin your product to a category's 1st or 3rd position, the spotlight, or the right sidebar. See Advertise.

On launch day

Share your page with your audience, reply to every comment and thank people who upvote. The most upvoted products rise to the top of the day, week and month.
