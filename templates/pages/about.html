About us

LetsPublic is a home for new products. We help makers get their first users, honest feedback and a little momentum on launch day.

What we believe

  • Makers come first. Launching should be simple, fair and fast, whether you are a solo founder or a small studio.
  • The community decides. Upvotes and likes, not budgets, decide what rises to the top of each day, week and month.
  • Ads are always labelled. Paid slots are clearly marked as Sponsored, and they never change organic rankings.

How it works

Submit your product, we review it, and it goes live right away or at the time you schedule. Want more reach? Choose a Premium launch or book an advertising slot. See the launch guide to get started.
