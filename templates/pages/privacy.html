Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what Launchly collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account data: username, email, name and display name.
  • Launch data: product details, logos and screenshots you submit, plus your upvotes and likes.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by Razorpay. We store order and payment IDs and amounts, never your card or UPI details.
  • Technical data: cookies for login, theme and currency preferences, and basic server logs.

How we use it

To run the service, review launches, send emails about your submissions (approval, requests for information), process payments, prevent abuse and improve the product.

Sharing

Product details and your display name are public once your launch is live. We share data with service providers only as needed to operate Launchly (for example payments, email and file storage). We do not sell personal data.

Retention and your rights

We keep your data while your account is active and as required for accounting. You can edit your profile in Settings, and you can ask us to access or delete your data by emailing {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
